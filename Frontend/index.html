PhishGuard

On-device-first phishing & scam detection for India's vernacular languages — because scams don't arrive in English.

Get the extension View source

The problem

Most phishing detectors are English-first. India loses thousands of crores to scams sent in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and code-mixed "Hinglish" — exactly the messages English filters miss. PhishGuard flags them in real time, on the page where they land.

How it works 3-tier escalation

Tier 0 · Heuristics

URL look-alike, punycode, IP & shortener checks plus multilingual scam-intent rules. Instant, on-device, explainable.

Tier 1 · On-device ML

A cross-script char-ngram classifier catches scams with no obvious keywords. Tiny, runs locally.

Fusion

An ML-led blend with a structural override turns both signals into one red / amber / green verdict.

Privacy

Reports are PII-redacted server-side before storage. OTPs, account numbers, names — stripped by construction.
